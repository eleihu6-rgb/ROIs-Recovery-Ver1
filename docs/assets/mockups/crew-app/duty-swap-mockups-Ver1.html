What the web does

The current portal flow, step by step

The web is a wide two-pane desk tool: a month calendar on the left, a tool panel on the right, and a 3-step Gantt wizard for new swaps. Setup lives on two separate sub-pages that most crews will touch once.

Submit = legalityRule check runs on submit. Fail: "Illegal · Others · 08–12 Oct · Rule 8004036 Basic Competency". Pass: request goes to crew B.
RecordsPending(OTH) → Pending(ADM) → Approved. Also Pending(ME), Withdrawn(OTH/ME), Unsuccess, Chanced, Canceled.

Test submit on TST: 392923 gives PR124/PR125 (08–11 Oct, A350, SEA) ↔ 447841 PR684/PR685 (A333, DOH). The server rejected it on crew B's side for Basic Competency (fleet), so no request was created and nothing reached 447841. The web gave no warning before submit, even though the fleet mismatch was visible in the tables. All three concepts below flag this before the crew presses send.

Shared by all three concepts

Setup, filters, results and the crew-B side

These screens behave the same whichever concept wins, so they are drawn once. Pop-ups use the crew-app Status-Card AppDialog (carrier-colour band, tone on the glyph and the primary pill).

Concept A

Swap Board: browse what others offer, then make an offer

A feed of unlocked duties from other crews, pre-filtered by "swap out of" one of my duties. Tapping a card opens an offer composer. Feels like a marketplace and is the fastest path when the crew already knows which trip they want to drop. On the Duo inner screen it becomes a board on the left and the composer on the right, split at the hinge.

Concept B

Twin Rail: the web Gantt rebuilt for touch

My duties sit on a pinned rail and each candidate crew gets a rail underneath, on the same day scale, so overlaps read at a glance. Tap blocks on both rails to build the swap; a tray at the bottom keeps the running delta. It stays closest to the web, which helps crews who use both. The Duo inner screen shows 7 rails and the review table side by side.

Concept C

Trade Ticket: a guided Give → Want → Match → Send flow

The crew first picks what to give, then what they want, and the app ranks matching crew+duty combos with a pre-check before anything is sent. The swap ends as a ticket with both sides, the KPI stub and the comment. It takes the most steps, but it gives the most guidance and works well one-handed. On the Duo inner screen the steps stay on the left and the live ticket builds on the right.

Concept D · from Ryan's sketch

Crew Matrix: dates down, crews across, scroll sideways to compare

A table with one row per date. Mine and Date stay frozen on the left; each candidate crew is a column on the right that scrolls left and right. Multi-day duties are vertical blocks spanning their rows, so a trip lines up against whatever each crew has on the same days. The rows of the duty I want to give are tinted across every column. Portrait shows 3 crews with a peek of the 4th; landscape shows 5–6. On the Duo inner screen a column border sits exactly on the hinge.

Recommendation: Concept A as the default compact flow, with B's twin rail as the review step's "Timeline" tab on the Duo inner screen. A is the cheapest to build and fits the existing tripTrade/MyDutyScreen list patterns. The rail adds the overlap view where the Duo inner screen has room for it.

With D added: D does the job of B's rail with a far simpler build (a frozen-column table instead of a touch timeline) and scales naturally from 3 to 6 crews with orientation. I would now pair A (find "out of" candidates) with D as the compare/pick screen, and drop B.
Open questions before coding
  • Does crew B's Accept re-run the legality check, and can B see the delta from their side? The web data suggests yes (Pending(ADM) follows) but I did not see a B session.
  • Is "Chanced" a typo for "Changed" (roster changed after approval)? The app should show "Changed".
  • Do we have a pre-check endpoint, or only submitTaskSwap? Without one, the fleet/rank warnings are client-side hints from cell data.
  • Push to crew B: is there a notification hook, or does B poll Records?
