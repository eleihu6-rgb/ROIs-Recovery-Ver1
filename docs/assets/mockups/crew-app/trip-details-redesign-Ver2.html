Design notes & data sources

A continuous journey sheet. The itinerary provides navigation. Duty preparation and totals sit alongside the flight tables on wide screens and above them on phones. All flights remain expanded; the layover stays between duties. The calendar action sits in the header.

The original examples contain local-time, duty-date and duration inconsistencies. These are retained for design comparison, not validated operational schedules. The rest interval is a sample portal value, not a legality determination.

Portal totals must be mapped from the captured payload into PortalDuty / Trip and persisted. Raw report/detailAll payloads are not stored. Where the F8/ET/EK mobile-roster feed supplies only check-in, release and blockMinutes, show Block and the duty span; omit unavailable FDP, DP and Rest rather than inventing values.

Boarding, door close, off-block, gate, stand and belt remain demo values until an A-CDM/AODB feed exists. Estimated-time fallbacks must be identified as filed schedule values. Calendar preview is illustrative and does not resolve the sample’s date inconsistencies.
