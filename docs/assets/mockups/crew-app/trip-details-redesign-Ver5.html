Crew App · Trip Details — operations ledger (Ver5)

One flat sheet instead of stacked cards: ruled rows, monospaced numerals, no shadows or rounded tiles inside the sheet. The masthead states the shared title and duty totals once; the six sectors read as a logbook. Denser than Ver3 — the same screen area carries the duty summary, the calendar action and every sector without scrolling.

Theme
1 · Shared duty title in the masthead

Flight-number string and routing appear once, above the ruled table — not on every sector.

2 · Paired times as columns

Scheduled / Estimated / Actual each hold one STD → STA pair, so a delay reads across the row.

3 · Duty totals in one ruled strip

FDP · DP · Block · Rest sit in the masthead and never grow with the sector count.

4 · Calendar as a ruled action row

One 32 pt line inside the sheet replaces the 50 pt card, keeping the calendar action visible without taking a column.

1 · iPhone — compact (420 × 912)

The sheet is the whole page. Each sector is a ruled block: flight line, three paired-time cells and one monospaced ops line. All six sectors and the masthead fit without scrolling on this screen.

1
Masthead

Duty id, report, shared title, routing and the four totals — one block, no repeated context below.

2
Three paired cells

Each cell holds a full pair; the row keeps STD/ETD/ATD aligned vertically across all six sectors.

3
Ops on one ruled line

Boarding · door close · off-block · gate/stand → belt in monospace, identically positioned on every block.

4
Calendar without a card

A single ruled action row inside the sheet — visible, tappable, and only 32 pt tall.

2 · iPhone Duo inner screen — wide (951 × 669)

The wide screen is the natural home of this style: seven columns, every sector on one line, ops on a second line. Six sectors, totals and the calendar action all fit at once.

2
Column alignment does the work

Tabular monospace keeps times stacked, so the eye compares ETD against STD down the column without reading labels.

5
Zebra + hairline rules

Alternating row tint and 1 px rules replace card shadows; density rises without visual noise.

6
Two-line row

The flight line and its ops line stay together; a sixth sector adds ~44 pt, not a new card.

7
One accent

Only the duty tag, the pair arrows and the block outline use the carrier accent; the rest is ink and hairlines.
